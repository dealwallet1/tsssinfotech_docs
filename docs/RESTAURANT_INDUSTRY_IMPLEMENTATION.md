# Restaurant Industry Solution - Initial Implementation

## Overview

Implemented the Restaurant Industry Solution by leveraging standard Odoo enterprise modules and configuring them to support restaurant business operations.

## Objective

Create a restaurant business workflow similar to the existing Industrial Solution by integrating Odoo's core business applications into a single restaurant-oriented implementation.

## Modules Configured

### Point of Sale
- Configured Restaurant POS
- Enabled Restaurant Mode
- Configured Floors & Tables
- Configured Dine-In, Takeaway, and Delivery
- Configured Payment Methods
- Tested Restaurant Order Flow

### Purchase
- Created Restaurant Vendors
- Configured Supplier Information
- Created Purchase Orders
- Validated Vendor Receipts

### Inventory
- Created Ingredient Products
- Managed Stock Availability
- Verified Inventory Operations
- Configured Product Categories

### Manufacturing
- Created Bills of Materials (Recipes)
- Configured Manufacturing Orders
- Validated Raw Material Consumption
- Verified Finished Product Production

### Product Management
- Created Restaurant Menu Categories
  - Biryani
  - Curries
  - Breads
  - Starters
  - Desserts
  - Beverages

- Created Menu Products
- Assigned POS Categories
- Added Product Images
- Configured Pricing

## Restaurant Workflow

Vendor
→ Purchase
→ Inventory
→ Recipe (BoM)
→ Manufacturing
→ Restaurant POS
→ Customer Order

## Current Progress

Completed the initial Restaurant Industry implementation by configuring and integrating:
