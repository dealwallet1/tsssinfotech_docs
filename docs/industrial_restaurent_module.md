- Configured vendor contact information.
- Linked vendors with ingredient products.

### Purchase Management
- Created purchase orders for restaurant ingredients.
- Validated purchase receipts.
- Updated inventory after receiving products.

### Inventory Management

- Created raw material products.

- Created Manufacturing Orders.
- Verified raw material consumption.
- Verified finished product creation.
